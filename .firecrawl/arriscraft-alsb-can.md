### [Winner of the 2023 Architectural Products Product Innovation Award!](https://blog.arriscraft.com/alsb-wins-2023-product-innovation-award/)

First introduced in 2018, Architectural Linear Series Brick (ALSB) filled a gap in the market for a distinct long brick only available from Europe. The distinctive weathered finish sets apart this brick from any other brick in the market. The purposeful weathering and blend of shorter and longer bricks (random lengths up to 23-5/8″) enhance the brick’s aesthetic.

This linear brick installs quickly and features the same fine grain texture as Arriscraft stone products. ALSB calcium silicate brick provides lifetime performance and doesn’t fade or discolor.

NOTE:

Skids are complete with up to 35% naturally fractured units, which should be used within the wall to achieve a random pattern, and for door, window openings etc. (Standard brick depth – 3-3/4″)
