King Masonry Yard Ltd. Supports Cancer Research

In 2014, we proudly donated 24,000 of our Dark Stockport Modular brick toward the 6,500 square foot Oakville Grand Prize Show home.

This gorgeous home was showcased in The Princess Margaret Cancer Centre Welcome Home Sweepstakes Lottery.
