## Details

Preston stone embodies the solid look of modern concrete stone veneer with a smooth face and clean lines in harmony with today’s design trends. Stocked in established, natural colours; Preston’s modular proportions make it the perfect companion for other metric brick and block in our wide range of masonry products.
